# Earthquakes and Tectonic Plates: Seismic Analysis
This project focuses on the seismic analysis of earthquakes and their relationship with tectonic plates. Earthquake data were analyzed to understand the spatial and temporal distribution of seismic events and their variation in terms of magnitude and depth. The analysis involved data preprocessing, exploratory data analysis, and visualization of important earthquake parameters such as latitude, longitude, magnitude, and depth. The project also examines the geographical distribution of earthquakes in relation to tectonic plate boundaries, helping to understand the connection between seismic activity and plate tectonics. Statistical visualizations were created using Matplotlib and Seaborn, while Folium was used to develop interactive maps and heatmaps for spatial analysis. The project demonstrates the application of Python-based data analysis and geospatial visualization techniques in geophysics.
